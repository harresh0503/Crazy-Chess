White's Turn

Crazy Chess is chess where your king goes on adventures to recruit an army, and every step forward is a gamble. In Crazy Chess, you don't start with a full army.You start with just your king on the board. To get other pieces (pawns, knights, bishops, rooks, queens), your king has to move into certain areas of the board to “recruit” them to fight your opponent. Instead of protecting your king while pieces fight, your king is the one going on missions to build the army. To learn more, visit "How to Play"
